Privacy

poghiamo is a personal, invite-only service that tracks concerts of artists you choose to follow. This page describes, plainly, what it stores and why.

What we store

Your username, a hash of your password (never the password itself), and your preferences: the artists you follow and the Italian regions you care about. Nothing else. No email address, no name, no analytics, no tracking of any kind.

Cookies

One strictly necessary session cookie keeps you logged in. It is not used for tracking and contains only an identifier of your session. No consent banner is required for this kind of cookie, and there are no other cookies.

Where

Everything is stored on a single server hosted by Hetzner in Germany (EU). Data never leaves it, is never shared, and is never sold.

Spotify

If you ever connect Spotify (an optional feature), the list of artists you follow there is imported and stored. Disconnecting removes that imported data immediately.

Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the settings page. Deletion is immediate and permanent, including everything attached to the account. Backups of the database rotate away within two weeks.

Contact

Questions or requests: ask the person who invited you, it is that kind of service.
